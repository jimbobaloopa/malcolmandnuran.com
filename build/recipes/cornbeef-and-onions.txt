title: Corned Beef and Onion
category: Curries + Sides

**Ingredients**

- 400g corned beef
- 1 tbls oil/ghee
- 1 red onion, sliced
- 1 medium tomato, diced
- 1 tsp ground coriander
- Tabasco and Worcestershire Sauce to taste

**Method**
Saute corned beef, onions and tomatoes in oil, add spice and sauce to taste.
Fry until cooked and combined.
