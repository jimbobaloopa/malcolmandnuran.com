Sausage Rolls

500gm sausage mince

2 sheets ready rolled puff pastry

1 medium brown onion

1 carrot

½ cup green coriander leaves

1 egg, beaten.

Peel and chop brown onion and carrot roughly. Put in a food processor together with the coriander leaves and zip it till minced up.Mix in with the sausage mince in a large bowl.

Divide the mince in 4 parts.

Cut the pastry sheets in half and brush the long ends with beaten egg.

Roll each portion of mince in a long sausage and place it lengthwise on the pastry sheets.

Roll the pastry over,tucking the ends to form a long sausage roll. Cut each roll into 4 small sausage rolls, brush the top with beaten egg and place on a oven tray lined with baking paper. If you havent got baking paper, grease the tray.

Bake in a hot oven, 200 C, for 20 minutes
