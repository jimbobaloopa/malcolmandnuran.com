Baked Fish

Baked Snapper Or Any Big Firm Fleshed Fish

In a baking tray place the clean fish

Stuff it if you like with the stuffing mix in a previous recipe

Squeeze 1 lemon juice over it,

Sprinkle with light soy sauce and sprigs of fresh coriander leaves

Dot it with a few bits of butter

Cover with foil and bake in oven at 170 degrees for ¾,s of an hour

In the meantime make lemon sauce that I wrote in another recipe and after ¾ hour lift the foil cover off the fish and pour the lemon sauce over it. bake uncovered for another ¼, hour or till cooked
