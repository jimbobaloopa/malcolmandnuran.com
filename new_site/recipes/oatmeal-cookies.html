Oatmeal Cookies

1 cup of Rolled Oats

¾ cup of coconut

1 cup of S.R. flour

1 teaspoon Carb Soda

1 Cup of Sugar

125g of butter

1 Tablespoon Golden Syrup

2 Tablespoons Boiling water

Mix dry ingredients together

Melt butter and syrup in small saucepan

Add water and Soda to melted butter/syrup and mix together

Stir till it froths up, remove from heat[careful not to burn yourself].

Add mixture to the dry ingredients. texture should not be runny

place about tablespoon of mixture rounded individually on greased tray or lined with baking paper.

Make sure you put it a fair bit apart as it will spread.

Bake in a slow oven at 150C (electric) for 20 minutes.

they will flatten out as cooking.

leave on tray tocool before removing them.It will firm up.
