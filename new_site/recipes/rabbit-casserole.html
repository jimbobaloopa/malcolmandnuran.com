Rabbit casserole

1 rabbit for 2 people

2 for 2 to 3 people

Soak the rabbit in salt water for 1 hour. remove and joint it

You’ll need some seasoned flour. Here’s how to make it.

1 cereal bowl of plain flour

2 teaspoons of salt

2 teaspoons of b.b.q. spice[ get it in woolies]

2 teaspoons of season all

2 teaspoons of freshly ground black pepper

1 teaspoon of smokey b.b.q.[ get it in a spice rack in green jar, in supermarket]

Mix all together and keep in a screw top jar. Use whenever needed. Good for beef casserole.

2 to 3 tablespoons seasoned flour.

Extra seasoned flour.

2 medium potatoes cut in halves

2 large carrots, chopped diagonally roughly.

1 stick celery sliced

½ red capsicum. cut in chunks

3 mushrooms sliced

1 brown onion sprig of sage, thyme, rosemary and oregano.zap altogether in a chopper or foodproceesor.

1 heaped teaspoon of crushed garlic

1 “ “ “ “ ginger.

½ tin diced tomatoes

1 teaspon of salt

½ to 1 cup of water

2 to 3 tablespoons of olive oil

Line a cereal bowl with greaseproof paper and pour in 2 to 3 tablespoon of seasoned flour. This saves the flour sticking to the bowl and easier to clean.

heat the olive oil in a large non stick fry pan on medium heat

Coat each rabbit piece with seasoned flour on both sides

and brown quickly on both sides. Remove and keep aside in a large casserole dishAdd more oil if needed and sauté the minced onion and herbs. add ginger and garlic and cook for a minute or so.

Add chopped potatoes, carrots, celery, capsicum and,mushrooms.

Stir fry for 2 minutes and then add 1 and ½ tablespoons of seasoned flour.

Stir and coat all the veggies with flour, add ½ tin tomatoes and water and salt.It should make a nice sauce. If too thick add a little more water, but not too much.

Pour over the rabbit and arrange the pieces till nicely mixed in with the sauce

Cover and bake in a slow oven, 160 degrees for 2 to 2 and ½ hours, depending on how big the rabbit is. All you need is steamed green vegy to eat it with.
