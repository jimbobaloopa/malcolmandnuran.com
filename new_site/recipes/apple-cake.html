Apple Cake

2 cups s.r. flour

1 cup sugar

2 eggs

2 teaspoon mixed spice or cinnamon

6 table.spoon melted margarine

2 peeled and grated apples

vanilla essence

sift flour and spice - add sugar, beaten eggs and vaanilla.

add melted margarine and grated apples.

mix all together with a wooden spoon and pour into a square or ring cake tin, lined with a glad bake paper and bake at 180 degrees for 30 minutes or so.

put a skewer through to test if it is cooked . if not cook it further five ten minutes with the temp. turned down to 170.

cool, cut into slice and butter.
