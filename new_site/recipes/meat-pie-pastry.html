Meat Pie Pastry

125 gms cold butter

2 ½ cups plain flour

Cold water

sift the plain flour giving it a lot of air,[hold the sieve high above the basin and shake it .

Grate the butter over the flour.

mix the butter in the flour with a knife

Sprinkle a little water at a time , mixing the dough with a knife till all the flour is combined, make sure it is not too wet.

Now with your hands bring the dough into a ball. If you need a bit more water, that’s fine.

The dough should come clean off the bowl

Wrap with a cling wrap and put in fridge for 20 minutes to rest

Roll it out to line a big pie dish or small individual ones.

Brush the edge of the pie with egg wash cover with a piece of thawed puff pastry,make a slit in the middle and brush with egg wash and bake in a hot oven 200 degrees for 20 minutes or till golden brown.

Various meat pie filling recipies will follow.
