White Sauce

White Sauce for Vegies,Cauliflower, or Corned Beef

2 Heaped tablespoons of lite butter or margarine

1 Tablespoon of plain flour

1 Teaspoon of crushed garlic

Salt nad White pepper

About a cup of lite milk

2 tablespoon of lite grated cheese or more.

In a small saucepan melt butter or margarine over low heat. Stir in crushed garlic. When the butter is melted, take off heat and mix in the plain flour. It should be a smooh paste. Put a little bit of milk and mix in. Put back on heat and add a little milk at a time, stiring all the time. Use a whisk to prevent lumps. Keep using the milk till it makes a smooth thick sauce.

Pour over a oven dish of precooked vegies, like zuccini, carrots, beans, or cauliflower and sprinkle with grated cheese and bake in a moderately hot oven for 15 mins or till the cheese has melted.

p.s. you can sprinke paprika over the cheese before putting in the oven. Or

mix the cheese in the sauce and use over cooked corned beef.

Serves two.
