Marinaded Wings and Kebabs

Chicken Kebabs or Marinated Chicken Winglets

For chicken kebabs;

K.G. of chicken breast

Can of pineapple pieces

1 Tablespoon of crushed garlic

1 Tablespoon of light soy sauce

1 Tablespoon of sweet chillie sauce

½ Tablespoon of soy honey and garlic sauce

½ Tablespoon of smokey b.b.q. sauce

½ teaspoon of sesame oil

Cut chicken breasts into cubes and marinate with the above mixture for half a day.

Thread chicken cubes alternating with pineapple pieces onto bamboo skewers.

Cook on the b.b.q. brushing with the marinade mixture as you turn the kebabs.

For chicken winglets;

Use the same marinade except cook the winglets on a lined tray in a hot oven turning them around to brown both sides.
