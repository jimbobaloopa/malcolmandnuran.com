Pizza pastry

3 Cups of S.R. Flour.

1 Teaspoon of Salt

1 Tablespoon of Olive Oil

1 Egg

Water

Add salt to flour. Add Olive oil and mix in an egg.

Using a knife add enough water to make a stiff dough.

Rest for half an hour before use
