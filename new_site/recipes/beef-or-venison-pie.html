Beef or Venison Pie

Steak And Mushroom Pie And Venison Pie

750 grms of diced steak /venison

5to6 mushrooms

1 br. onion

Sprig of sage, rosemary, thyme

Worecester sauce

Season all, b.b.q. seasoning and ground black pepper

Heaped teaspoon crushed ginger

“ “ “ garlic

½ to 1 cup red wine

gravox to thicken

2 tablespoons olive oil

Process the br. onion and herbs together

Heat up the olive oil in a heavy based saucepan

Saute the onions and herbs , garlic and ginger

Stir fry for 2 minutes then add the mushrooms

Turn the heat up high and add the diced steak/venison

Add the seasonings [no salt as you will thicken with gravox later and that is salty enough ]

Stir and fry for 5 minutes on high heat, make sure the meat is sealed up

Add about tablespoon of worcester sauce and fry for another minute or so.

Add enough red wine to cover [you can add dark ale or guiness instead of red wine if making a guiness pie]

Bring to boil, cover, turn down the heat and simmer for an hour and half or till the meat is tender and the stock is reduced.

Add 1 tablespoon of gravox mixed with some more Worcester sauce and water.

Stir into the cooked meat till it is thick, if too dry add a bit more water

Taste to see if all the seasoning is right and take off the heat.

Cool before putting into a lined pie dish with the meat pie pastry that I wrote earlier on. Cover with thawed puff pastry, brush with egg wash, make a slit in the middle, and bake in a hot oven 200 degrees for 20 minutes or till golden brown.
