Lemon Sauce

Lemon Sauce for fish, especially for salmon

¼ cup white wine

¾ cup chicken stock

½ lemon juice

Boil all in a small saucepan till reduced to half

Then whisk in 30 gms of unsalted butter

Mix 1 teaspoon of cornflour and ½ teaspoon of sugar or sweetner in a little water.

whisk in with the sauce, it should thicken nicely. taste to see if it is not too sour. if it is , add a little more sugar. lastly add a few sprigs of fresh coriander and serve over cooked fish
