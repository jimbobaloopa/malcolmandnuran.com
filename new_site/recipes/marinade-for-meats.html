Marinade for Meats

Marinated Meats for B.B.Q.

Rump Steak or any other boneless beef steaks.

Lamb Chops and Kangaroo Steaks

Crushed garlic.

Marinade Mixture for each kg.of meat:

1 Dessert spoon Dark Soy Sauce

1 Dessert spoon Hoi Sin Sauce

1 Dessert spoon Masterfoods Smokey B.B.Q. Sauce

Method :

Rub crushed garlic on the meat and marinate the various meats with the marinade mixture for at least 2 to 3 hours before hand

However I prefer to leave them overnight in a covered dish in the fridge.
