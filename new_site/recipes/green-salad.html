Nuran's Recipes · Salads

Green salad

Half lettuce sliced finely

Half a red onion, diced finely

¼ red capsicum diced

Half continental cucumber sliced half lengthwise then quartered and then chopped finely

Dash of garlic salt

White pepper

2 teaspoon of extra virgin olive oil

1 tablespoon of Italian salad dressing

Toss and serve straight away.
