Nuran's Recipes

One of my mum's passions is to cook.
Dad likes to take the credit for mum's ability to cook. He says that he taught her how to cook although his contribution was the fact that he CAN'T cook! (Except for a tasty steak on the barbie).
So mum got plenty of practice cooking all the time for dad and her four sons

Mum gets great pleasure seeing her concoctions being devoured by her sons and if we are feeling nice and want to make her even more happy we might go back for seconds!
(just to please her of course)

Well now you can benefit from mum's culinary skills as she has provided a select few of her most sought after recipes.

Just choose a recipe from the list, cook and enjoy.
