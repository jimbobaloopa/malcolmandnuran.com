Tinned salmon salad

Recipe for tinned salmon salad

1 425g.m. tinned salmon

3 spring onions

3 sprig coriander

1 small tomatoe

lemon and lime juice

garlic salt and white pepper.

Tip the tinned salmon into a bowl. Chop up spring onions, coriander and finely dice tomatoe. Mix in the salmon with lemon and lime juice and salt and pepper with a fork.

Serve with steamed potatoes and green salad or coleslaw.

A very nice budget meal with omega 3
