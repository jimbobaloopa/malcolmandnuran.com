Coriander Chicken

2 skinless chicken breast, sliced

2 teasp. crushed garlic

sprinkle of sesame oil

2 teasp. light soy sauce

2 to 3 teasp cornflour

1 egg white

1 cup of coriander leaves chopped

2 tbsp.cream

2tbsp olive or canola oil

mix the chicken and marinade except for the coriander and cream

leave for an hour

in a non stick frypan heat the oil fairly hot. cook the chicken stirring all the time so it doesn’t stick till the chicken is cooked. add the coriander leaves and half cup of water with 2 teaspoons of cornflour mixed in

add the cream and take off the heat

serve with plain boiled rice and salad
