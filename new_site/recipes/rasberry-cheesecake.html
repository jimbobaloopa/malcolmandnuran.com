Rasberry Cheesecake

White Chocolate and Raspberry Cheesecake

1 ¼ cups shortbread biscuits crushed.

75 g butter melted

500g blocks Philadelphia Cream Cheese softened

¾ cup sugar

1 teaspoon grated lemon rind

2 teaspoons gelatine dissolved in ¼ cup of cooled boiled water.

200g white chocolate meted

1 cup cream lightly whipped

½ cup frozen fresh raspberries 200g frozen fresh raspberries, extra

icing sugar

Combine the biscuits and butter and press into the base of a lightly greased 22cm spring form pan

Beat the Cream Cheese, sugar and lemon rind with an electric mixer until smooth. stir the gelatine mixture and fold in the melted choc., cream and ½ cup of raspberries

Pour the filling into the prepared base. Refrigerate 3 hours or until set. garnish with extra raspberries and dust with icing sugar.
