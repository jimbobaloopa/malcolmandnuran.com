Three Bean salad

Three Bean Mix Salad

425g Can of 3 Bean Mix

1 to 2 Tablespoons of finely chopped red onions.

1 Teaspoon of mixed herbs

1 Tablespoons of olive oil

2 Teaspoons of dark Balsamic Vinegar

2 Tablespoons of tomato sauce

1 dessertspoon of sweet chillie sauce

! dessertspoon of Hot Chilli sauce

A few drops of tabasco sauce

Garlic salt to taste

White pepper

Mix all the sauce ingredients together, drain the tinned beans and toss in the sauce. Put covered in fridge before use.
