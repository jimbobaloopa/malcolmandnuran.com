Hamburgers

hamburgers {beef or chicken]

1 kg of lean beef mince

Or 3 whole chicken breast skinless, diced and minced in the food processor.

2 Large carrots, chopped roughly

1 Large onion, quartered

1 Cup of coriander leaves

2 to 3 cloves garlic

2-3 large frozen brown bread

If fresh toast them

2 tablespoons of plain flour

2 eggs

b.b.q spice

all purpose seasoning

white pepper

Chicken salt. [put about teaspoon of each]

put carrots, onion, coriander and garlic in food processor and mince them

take it out and put on the mince together with the plain flour,

break the frozen bread, 2 at a time and food process them till they form crumbs

Add them to the mince together with the eggs and seasonings,

Mix them by hand till well combined, if they are a bit wet, add a little more bread crumbs

Roll them into large balls and roll them in fine bread crumbs.

Cut out a square of cling wrap, put the mince ball on one side, cover over with the rest of the cling wrap and press flat to form a hamburger. wrap over the other two edges and you have a wrapped up hamburger. stack in fridge or use as fresh.

Keep them wrapped up till ready to cook.
